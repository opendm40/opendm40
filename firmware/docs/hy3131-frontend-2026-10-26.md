# HY3131 front end: pre-filter capacitor, AC coupling, AC V compensation switches (2026-10-26)

One unit (UID 1E6830202422B30736313039), image from branch `exp/hy-poke` with
`DBG:HY:CAP?` ([`src/hyexp.rs`](../src/hyexp.rs); host helper
[`tools/experiments/hycap.py`](../tools/experiments/hycap.py)). Source: the
MR9270S+ on V/Ω–COM (DC voltage; the pulse output in its kHz range, a 0 / ~10 V
square up to 100 kHz, frequency checked with the DM40 counter). Data:
[`data/hy-frontend-2026-10-26/`](data/hy-frontend-2026-10-26/).

`DBG:HY:CAP? kind,n,npre{,i,v}` writes the row the DMM last wrote with the
given bytes (i < 22: R20 + i), drops `npre` results, writes the step bytes
(i = 32 + byte) one SPI write each, then returns n results (AD1, RMS or AD2)
with the time since the step. The DMM row is restored afterwards.

## 1. FTP / FTN pre-filter capacitor: ~26 nF

DC V 6 V, calibrator 1 V and 3 V. AD1 at R22 = 0x00 (OSR 256, unchopped,
1.56 ms per result). Step by SPI: R24 0x21 (AD1FP = AD1FN = RLU, zero) ->
0x31 (OP1O - RLU), R33 SFT1 set in the pre row.

| SFT1 | pre-filter R | settling |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | open (capacitor off) | 2 results (the decimation filter) |
| 10 | 0 | 2 results |
| 01 | 10 k | ~1 result more |
| 00 | 100 k | exponential, τ = 2.54..2.59 ms (6 runs, 1 V and 3 V) |

In DS §12.4 (p.27) the 100 k / 10 k resistor is in the AD1FP leg only and the
capacitor sits between FTP (after it) and FTN (on AD1FN), so τ = 100 k · C:
C ≈ 25.6 nF (the on-chip resistor's tolerance is not specified). The Hycon
demo board fits 27 nF (C12, APD-DMM001 p.22).

The slow "memory" of the ohm 6 MΩ / 60 MΩ paths in `DBG:SRC` (SFT1 = 00) is
then the source impedance charging this capacitor (10 MΩ · 26 nF ≈ 0.26 s
against 50 ms conversions) (*inferred*, not measured separately).

## 2. AC V coupling: τ = 0.34 s

AC V 6 V; AD1 on PB4 - AGND (R24 0xC3, SFT1 = 11, R22 = 0x02: OSR 1024
unchopped, 6.25 ms), calibrator DC 0 -> 1 V during the capture. PB4 jumps and
decays as one exponential: τ = 338..347 ms (fit from 0.03..0.6 s after the
peak, residual < 0.1 % of the step). This gives R · C only: the demo board's
1 µF + 1 MΩ would be 1 s; which R and C the DM40 fits is not known.

## 3. AC V compensation switches (FS9, FS1) and the ACC array

The stock AC V rows set FS1 (R2A 0x20) and FS9 + PS8 (R2E 0x28) on every
range; the tap is PS6 / SS6 on 6 V and 60 V (the same tap, OP1 gain differs)
and PS5 / SS5 on 600 V. SMODE5 is set (R2F 0xA0), ACC = 0 (R30 0x80).

RMS results (R09..R0D, √ of 4 results after 4 dropped) of the square with one
change to the row, relative to the stock row, same source level:

| f | 6 V: FS9 off | FS1 off | ACC 127 | 600 V: FS9 off | FS1 off | ACC 127 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 kHz | +0.43 % | +0.02 % | 0.00 % | +0.38 % | -0.02 % | 0.00 % |
| 1 kHz | +0.79 % | +0.03 % | 0.00 % | +0.74 % | -0.01 % | -0.01 % |
| 5 kHz | +3.52 % | +0.20 % | 0.00 % | +3.54 % | +0.08 % | 0.00 % |
| 10 kHz | +7.17 % | +0.45 % | 0.00 % | +7.08 % | +0.24 % | 0.00 % |
| 20 kHz | +17.6 % | +1.8 % | 0.00 % | +17.0 % | +1.1 % | +0.03 % |
| 30 kHz | +29.3 % | +4.4 % | 0.00 % | - | - | - |

(60 V repeats 6 V to 0.1 %. ACC 16 / 64 change nothing either, and ACC 127
with FS9 off moves the FS9-off result by at most 0.3 %; above ~40 kHz the RMS result of this square is at the noise
floor, so higher frequencies say nothing.) The spread of 4 results is
< 0.01 % up to 20 kHz.

- FS9 is in the signal path's frequency response: switched off, the HF
  content rises, by the same amount on the 101 k (6 V) and the 10 k (600 V)
  tap. So its element does not load the divider tap; it sits at a node whose
  impedance does not change with the range (*inferred*).
- The square's fundamental, fitted from raw AD2 samples (reads every ~47 µs,
  snapped to the 13.02 µs conversion grid), rises with FS9 off by +0.46 % at
  2 kHz, +3.0 % at 5 kHz and +11 % at 10 kHz (roughly ∝ f², like a pole near
  20 kHz added by FS9) (`fund_6.csv`; above 10 kHz this fit scatters by 3..25 %).
  With FS9 on the fundamental is -1.5 % at 5 kHz and -10 % at 10 kHz against
  1 kHz, with FS9 off +1.4 % and -0.3 %. That includes the AD2 decimation
  filter, whose response is not documented, so which setting is flatter at
  the input cannot be told from this.
- The ACC array does nothing measurable in the AC V rows (SMODE5 on); where its
  capacitors connect on the DM40 is not known.
- FS1 has a much smaller effect of the same sign (about 1/15 of FS9 at 10 kHz).
